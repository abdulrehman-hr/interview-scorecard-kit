# Interview Question Bank

60+ questions organized by category. Pick 6-8 per interview and ask every
candidate the same core set. Each question includes **what to listen for**
and common **red flags**.

---

## A. Opening / Warm-Up (2-3 min)

1. **Tell me about yourself.**
   - Listen for: A concise 60-90 second professional summary, not their life story.
   - Red flag: Rambling with no structure, or jumping straight to salary.

2. **What do you know about our company?**
   - Listen for: Evidence they did homework — our work, our clients, our values.
   - Red flag: "Not much" or generic praise.

3. **Why are you looking to leave your current role?** (or: Why did you leave your last role?)
   - Listen for: Forward-looking reasons (growth, learning). Honest but professional.
   - Red flag: Trash-talking a former employer.

---

## B. Experience & Background

4. **Walk me through your proudest professional achievement.**
   - Listen for: Specifics — their role, the challenge, measurable outcome.
   - Red flag: Vague claims with no numbers or personal contribution.

5. **What does a typical day look like in your current role?**
   - Listen for: Concrete responsibilities that match the CV.
   - Red flag: Big mismatch with what the CV claims.

6. **What skills are you currently working on improving?**
   - Listen for: Self-awareness and a real development plan.
   - Red flag: "Nothing" or a disguised strength ("I work too hard").

---

## C. Behavioral Questions (ask for STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result)

7. **Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager. What did you do?**
   - Listen for: Respectful pushback, data-driven argument, acceptance of the final call.
   - Red flag: "I've never disagreed" (unlikely) or going over the manager's head.

8. **Describe a difficult deadline you had to meet. How did you handle it?**
   - Listen for: Prioritization, communication under pressure, outcome.
   - Red flag: Blaming others for the pressure.

9. **Tell me about a mistake you made at work and what you learned.**
   - Listen for: Ownership, specific lesson, changed behavior afterward.
   - Red flag: Blame-shifting or "I can't think of one."

10. **Give an example of a time you had to learn something completely new, fast.**
    - Listen for: Learning method, speed, and result.
    - Red flag: No example — suggests low adaptability.

11. **Tell me about a conflict with a colleague and how you resolved it.**
    - Listen for: Empathy, direct communication, resolution.
    - Red flag: "I get along with everyone" (avoids the question).

12. **Describe a time you improved a process or suggested a better way of doing things.**
    - Listen for: Initiative, measurable improvement.
    - Red flag: Never having suggested anything.

---

## D. Situational / Hypothetical

13. **It's your first week. Your manager is on leave and a client issue lands on your desk. What do you do?**
    - Listen for: Structured thinking — assess, escalate appropriately, document.
    - Red flag: "I'd wait for the manager to return."

14. **You discover a colleague is cutting corners in a way that affects quality. What do you do?**
    - Listen for: Addresses it directly first, escalates if needed, cares about standards.
    - Red flag: "Not my problem."

15. **You have two urgent tasks and time for only one. How do you decide?**
    - Listen for: Asks about impact/deadlines, communicates with stakeholders.
    - Red flag: Guessing without asking for information.

16. **A customer is angry about something that isn't your fault. Walk me through the conversation.**
    - Listen for: Listening, empathy, ownership of resolution (not blame).
    - Red flag: Arguing or getting defensive.

---

## E. Role-Specific: HR & People Roles

17. **Walk me through your end-to-end recruitment process for a hard-to-fill role.**
    - Listen for: Sourcing strategy, screening method, stakeholder management, metrics.
    - Red flag: "I just post on LinkedIn and wait."

18. **How do you handle a hiring manager who keeps rejecting good candidates?**
    - Listen for: Data-driven calibration, honest conversation, process fixes.
    - Red flag: Siding entirely with one party.

19. **What HR metrics do you track, and which one matters most to you?**
    - Listen for: Time-to-hire, quality-of-hire, turnover, offer acceptance — with reasoning.
    - Red flag: "I don't really track metrics."

20. **How do you ensure compliance with EOBI/PESSI and labor regulations?**
    - Listen for: Concrete process — registrations, filings, audits, record-keeping.
    - Red flag: Vague "HR handles that" with no specifics.

21. **Tell me about a difficult termination or disciplinary case you managed.**
    - Listen for: Fair process, documentation, empathy, legal care.
    - Red flag: Enjoying the power, or skipping due process.

---

## F. Culture & Values Fit

22. **What kind of work environment brings out your best?**
    - Listen for: Alignment with your actual environment.
    - Red flag: Wants the opposite of what you offer.

23. **How do you like to receive feedback?**
    - Listen for: Openness, examples of acting on feedback.
    - Red flag: "I don't need much feedback."

24. **What would your last team say about working with you?**
    - Listen for: Self-awareness; ideally matches what references would say.
    - Red flag: Only superlatives, no nuance.

25. **What matters more to you right now: title, money, or learning? Why?**
    - Listen for: Honest prioritization that fits the role's reality.
    - Red flag: Answer clearly mismatched to what the role offers.

---

## G. Motivation & Career Goals

26. **Where do you see yourself in 3 years?**
    - Listen for: Ambition with realism; growth direction.
    - Red flag: No idea, or a plan that clearly doesn't involve this job.

27. **What motivates you on a difficult Monday morning?**
    - Listen for: Intrinsic drivers — mastery, purpose, team.
    - Red flag: Only extrinsic ("payday").

28. **Why this role, and why now?**
    - Listen for: Specific connection between their goals and this exact role.
    - Red flag: Generic answer that fits any job.

---

## H. Closing (candidate's turn)

29. **What questions do you have for me?**
    - Listen for: Thoughtful questions about the role, team, success metrics.
    - Red flag: No questions at all, or only about perks/leave.

30. **Is there anything about your background you'd like to clarify?**
    - Listen for: Proactive honesty (gaps, short stints).
    - Red flag: Defensive or evasive.

---

## Questions to NEVER ask

Avoid anything about: age, marital status, pregnancy/family plans, religion,
ethnicity, disability, or political views. In Pakistan, as elsewhere, these
expose the company legally and tell you nothing about job performance. If a
candidate volunteers such info, don't follow up on it — steer back to the role.

## Tips for interviewers

- Ask the **same core questions** to every candidate for the role.
- Take notes on **evidence**, not adjectives ("gave a 3-step example" > "seems smart").
- Score **immediately after** the interview, before discussing with others.
- One interviewer, one scorecard — compare only after everyone has scored.
